Northwind Labs — Senior Full Stack Engineer (Remote, US)

Northwind Labs builds supply-chain visibility software for mid-market manufacturers. Our platform tracks 2 million shipments a month and our customers use it to predict delays before they happen.

About the role
We're hiring a Senior Full Stack Engineer to join the Customer Platform team. You'll own features end to end — from the React front end to Node.js services and the PostgreSQL schemas behind them — and help us bring LLM-powered insights into the product.

What you'll do
- Build and ship customer-facing features in React, TypeScript and Next.js
- Design APIs (REST and GraphQL) and data models in PostgreSQL
- Partner with our ML team to productionize LLM features: retrieval, summaries, anomaly explanations
- Raise the bar on performance, accessibility and testing
- Mentor engineers and lead technical design reviews

What we're looking for
- 5+ years building production web applications
- Deep experience with React and TypeScript; solid Node.js
- Experience with PostgreSQL and API design
- Bonus: logistics or supply-chain domain experience; shipped LLM features; AWS

Compensation: $150,000 – $185,000 base + equity. Fully remote within the US.
